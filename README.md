# Sadness Intro

**Version:** 1.2  
**Development:** 2022–2023  
**Platform:** Commodore Plus/4  
**Language:** 6502 assembly  
**Assembler:** KickAssembler  
**Status:** Completed and tested; archived for preservation

## About

An intro named after its soundtrack, *Sadness in Your Eyes*. A large hi-res logo moves horizontally according to a sine table. Two 1×2 character scrollers are included: greetings at the top and information at the bottom.

The program attempts to auto-detect a SID card, although detection is not completely reliable. If no sound is heard, press **S** to force the playback selection.

## Music credits

- **Sadness in Your Eyes** — **Ari-Pekka Paljakka (Zardax)**, Origo Dreamline (1993)

The original C64 SID tune supports SID-card and TED playback.

## Building

The sources have been converted to **KickAssembler**. Earlier references to DASM, as65 or Plus4IDE describe historical development tools, not current build requirements. The original source files are preserved without modification.
